Lemon Tea

Description

Lemon tea is a refreshing drink made with black tea, fresh lemon juice, and a little sugar or honey.

Ingredients

  • Water
  • Black tea
  • Fresh lemon
  • Sugar or honey

Steps

  1. Boil the water.
  2. Add the black tea and let it steep for a few minutes.
  3. Remove the tea leaves or tea bag.
  4. Add fresh lemon juice.
  5. Add sugar or honey according to your taste.
  6. Stir well and serve hot.
