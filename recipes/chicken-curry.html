Chicken Curry

Description

Chicken curry is a flavorful dish made with tender chicken, onions, tomatoes, and a blend of aromatic spices.

Ingredients

  • Chicken
  • Onion
  • Tomatoes
  • Garlic
  • Ginger
  • Cooking oil
  • Turmeric
  • Chili powder
  • Garam masala
  • Salt
  • Water

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a pan.
  2. Add the onions and cook until golden.
  3. Add ginger and garlic and cook for a minute.
  4. Add the tomatoes and spices.
  5. Add the chicken and mix well.
  6. Add water and cover the pan.
  7. Cook until the chicken is completely cooked.
  8. Serve hot with rice or bread.
