Lasagna Recipe

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, creamy cheese, and tomato sauce. It is baked until the cheese is melted and golden.

Ingredients

  • Lasagna noodles
  • Ground beef
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Garlic
  • Ricotta cheese
  • Mozzarella cheese
  • Parmesan cheese
  • Olive oil
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Italian seasoning

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Cook the lasagna noodles according to the package instructions.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan and cook the onion and garlic.
  4. Add the ground beef and cook until browned.
  5. Add the tomato sauce, salt, pepper, and Italian seasoning. Simmer the sauce for a few minutes.
  6. Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  7. Add a layer of lasagna noodles followed by ricotta and mozzarella cheese.
  8. Repeat the layers until all the ingredients are used.
  9. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan cheese.
  10. Bake for about 30–40 minutes until the cheese is melted and golden.
  11. Let the lasagna cool for a few minutes before serving.
